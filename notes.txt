PROJECT ID - 78G0OL

PROBLEM STATEMENT
    Analyze and Provide Insights on Amazon Sales Report

DESCRIPTION 
    Dataset contains information about Amazon sales transactions.
    It contains:
        - Order ID
        - Date 
        - Status
        - Fulfilment
        - Sales Channel
        - ship-service-level
        - Category
        - Courier Status
        - Size 
        - Qty
        - currency
        - ship-city
        - ship-state
        - ship-postal-code
        - ship-country
        - Amount
        - B2B
        - fulfilled-by
        - New
        - PendingS

OBJECTIVE
    - Sales Overview: Analyze overall sales trends and patterns over time.
    - Product Analysis: Identify popular product categories, sizes, and quantities.
    - Fulfillment Analysis: Evaluate effectiveness of fulfillment methods.
    - Customer Segmentation: Segment customers based on behavior and location.
    - Geographical Analysis: Study sales distribution across regions.
    - Business Insights: Provide actionable recommendations for growth.

NOTES 
    - Sales Overview
        - Questions
            - What is the total sales revenue?
            - How many orders were placed?
            - Which month/day had the highest sales?
            - Are sales increasing or decreasing over time?
            - Are there seasonal trends?
        - Example Insight
            - Sales increased by 18% from April to June, indicating growing customer demand.
        - Insights

    - Product Analysis
        - Questions
            - Which product category sells the most?
            - Which product category generates the most revenue?
            - Which size is most purchased?
            - What quantity is usually ordered?
            - Which products have low sales?
        - Example Insight
            - Clothing category contributes 45% of total revenue, while XXL size has the lowest demand.
        - Insights

    - Fulfillment Analysis
        - Questions
            - Which fulfillment method is used most?
            - Does Amazon Fulfilled have fewer cancellations?
            - Which method generates more revenue?
            - Which method has the highest return rate?
        - Example Insight
            - Amazon Fulfillment handles 72% of orders and shows a lower cancellation rate compared to Merchant Fulfillment.
        - Insights

    - Customer Segmentation
        - Questions
            - Which states/cities order the most?
            - Who are the high-value customers?
            - Which locations place frequent orders?
            - Are some regions buying specific products?
        - Example Insight
            - Customers from Maharashtra and Karnataka contribute over 35% of total sales revenue.
        - Insights

    - Geographical Analysis
        - Questions
            - Which states have the highest sales?
            - Which regions have low sales?
            - Where should marketing efforts be focused?
            - Which locations have high cancellation rates?
        - Example Insight
            - Southern states account for the largest share of sales, while North-Eastern states show untapped growth potential.
        - Insights

    - Business Insights and recommendations
